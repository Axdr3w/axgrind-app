AX NUTRITION RULES
1
Protein first, every meal
Hit your protein goal before anything else. It keeps you full, protects muscle, and makes everything easier.
2
Time your carbs around training
Eat most carbs before and after your workout — that's when your body actually uses them.
3
1 gallon of water minimum
Performance, recovery, and body comp all suffer when you're dehydrated. No excuses.
4
Track everything for 2 weeks
Use MyFitnessPal. You can't manage what you don't measure. Just 2 weeks will change your perspective.
VITAMINS & MINERALS YOU NEED
The essentials for recovery & performance

⚠️ General nutrition education, not personalized medical advice. Everyone's needs are different — talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about yours, especially while you're still growing.

☀️
Vitamin D
Bone strength, immune function, hormone support. Easy to run low on if you're mostly indoors.
Sunlight, fatty fish, egg yolks, fortified milk
⚡
Vitamin B12
Energy production, red blood cells, nerve function.
Meat, eggs, dairy, fortified cereal
🩸
Iron
Carries oxygen to your muscles and brain — low iron means constant fatigue.
Red meat, spinach, beans, fortified cereal
🦴
Calcium
Bone density and muscle contraction — critical while your skeleton is still developing.
Dairy, leafy greens, fortified plant milk
💤
Magnesium
Muscle recovery, sleep quality, fewer cramps.
Nuts, seeds, dark chocolate, leafy greens
🛡️
Zinc
Immune defense, muscle repair, healthy hormone levels.
Meat, shellfish, pumpkin seeds, legumes
🐟
Omega-3s
Lowers inflammation, supports joints and heart health.
Salmon, walnuts, flaxseed, fish oil
🍊
Vitamin C
Immune support, collagen production, helps you absorb iron.
Citrus, bell peppers, strawberries, broccoli
🍌
Potassium
Muscle function and fluid balance — helps prevent cramps.
Bananas, potatoes, avocado, beans
🌾
Fiber
Digestion, gut health, keeps you full longer.
Oats, vegetables, fruit, whole grains
FOODS: PRIORITIZE VS LIMIT
✓ Get more of
Lean protein — chicken, turkey, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt
Complex carbs — oats, rice, sweet potato, quinoa
Healthy fats — avocado, olive oil, nuts, fatty fish
Colorful vegetables — spinach, broccoli, peppers, carrots
Whole fruit — berries, bananas, oranges, apples
Water — aim for that gallon a day
✗ Try to limit
Sugary drinks & soda
Deep-fried food
Heavily processed snacks & candy
Excess added sugar
Energy drinks — not appropriate for a growing body
Trans fats in packaged baked goods
